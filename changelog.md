# v1.0.0
- First version: macro loading (GDR, GDR2, Mega Hack JSON, TASBot, zBot, ReplayEngine 3, Silicate 1, txt, xd), frame-window scan, markers, counter, sub-frame list.

# v1.1.0
- Counter goes up as you hit each click
- Circles pop up where you click/release (only releases that matter)
- "1 frames: N" in the top right and as a banner every attempt

# v1.2.0
- Ding sound when you hit a frame perfect click or release (setting: how hard, or your own sound)
- Counter only goes up once you're past the click you hit
- Counter rows and where the counter ends are now settings (e.g. 15-30, 31+)

# v1.3.0
- Clicks are now caught on the player itself and matched by position or time, so circles, dings and the counter react to your clicks
- Counter goes up the moment you hit a click
- Ranges editor in the FI menu (pick which frame counts get their own row)
- Banner says "<level> has N one frames"
- Optional click info line for troubleshooting
- New scans store more info per click (old scans still work, rescanning is better)
